Unit 5: Derivatives

Unit 5

Derivatives Live

Live games on one host screen. For each derivative, run the story game first (one student per iPad, Kahoot style), then the trading desk (one iPad per group, six rounds, you reveal what happened). Futures: Futures Face-Off (a 15-question warm-up review to open class), then The Prom Bus. Options: The Sneaker Drop, The Options Floor, then The Options Floor, Part 2 (six stocks with company analysis and an economy report each round, getting harder each round). In both Options Floor games, desks work out their own result after each reveal and see if they got it right. Swaps: The Tip Jar Trade, The Swap Desk. CDS: The Cousin Loan, The Default Desk.

Futures Live

Kahoot-style futures wrap-up after The Prom Bus, one student per iPad. Four rounds: the deal, picking a side, gain or loss at delivery, and whether the hedge worked. Ends with the Top 5, class results and a "Reteach first" list.

 Unit 4

Bond Ladder

Unit 4 review at tables of 3, one student per iPad. You pick the rounds: Rating Room issuers, Bond Desk years and mystery yield curves. Each round is Quick Fire questions plus one main task. Ladder moves (winner up, 2nd stays, last down) or regroup by place. Ends with the Top 5 and class results.

 Unit 4

Recession Radar Live

The yield curve game. Ten real Treasury curves from mystery dates, two of them traps: groups name the shape, call the timing, and bet chips on iPads. Three Spot the Analyst rounds mix each group's one-sentence case with a real analyst's. You reveal the date and what happened. Ends with today's curve, the class forecast and the what-if simulator.
