The scorecard (0 to 4 points per factor)
Earnings = money left after operating costs, before interest. For a city, school district, or authority, it is the surplus. Use the most recent year (2026). Revenue change = (2026 revenue − 2022 revenue) ÷ 2022 revenue. All issuers are made up; real rating agencies use many more factors and their own judgment.
