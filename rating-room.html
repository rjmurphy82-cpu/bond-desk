Key words
Earnings = money left after operating costs, before interest. For a city, school district, or authority, this is its surplus. Use the most recent year (2026).
Debt due next year = the part of its debt it must repay in the next 12 months.
Revenue change = (2026 revenue − 2022 revenue) ÷ 2022 revenue.
All companies and governments in The Rating Room are made up. Real rating agencies (Moody's, S&P, Fitch) use many more factors and their own judgment; this scorecard is a simplified version of how they think.
