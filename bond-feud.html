Bond Feud

The Bond Desk, The Rating Room and the Yield Curve in one review game. Name your teams and go.

Number of teams:

Part 1: Feud Boards (6)

  • Two teams face off each board (rotate pairs so all 8 teams play). First good answer controls the board.
  • Type a guess (or click a tile) to reveal it. Wrong guess = a strike.
  • Three strikes: the other team gets one steal for the whole bank.
  • Boards 4 and 5 are worth double. Board 6 is triple.

Part 2: Lightning Round

  • 18 questions, 20 seconds each, four colored answers.
  • Every team answers on a whiteboard or by holding up a color.
  • Reveal, then click every team that got it right (+10).
  • Keys: Space = reveal / next, 1 to 6 = flip tile, X = strike.
